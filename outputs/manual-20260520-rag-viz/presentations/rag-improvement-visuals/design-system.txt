Slide size: 1280x720

Background system

- Warm paper base: #F6F1E8
- Occasional deep ink band: #13202A

Typography

- Display/claim: Apple SD Gothic Neo, semibold or bold
- Labels and body: Apple SD Gothic Neo
- Fallback: Arial

Palette

- Ink: #13202A
- Muted ink: #4B5963
- Deep blue: #0B5D7E
- Green: #2A7A5E
- Rust: #C7553A
- Gold: #C8952C
- Pale blue: #DDEBF2
- Pale green: #DDECE3
- Pale rust: #F1DED8
- Hairline: #B9C1C7

Chart and diagram grammar

- Use editable primitives, not screenshots.
- Use thick left-to-right flows with compact labels.
- Use hairline rules and lanes instead of decorative card grids.
- Use direct labels instead of legends.

Footer/source grammar

- Every slide has a small source/note footer.
- Sources are named quietly, not as large logos.

Banned motifs

- No pseudo logos.
- No decorative icons that imply a brand identity.
- No one-note blue/purple dashboard theme.
- No repeated three-card layout cadence.
